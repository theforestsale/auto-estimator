Questions, answered

Who actually writes the estimate?

You describe the customer's complaint, the AI drafts a diagnosis, priced work list, and a message to the customer. You review it before sending — nothing leaves your account automatically.

Is the estimate accurate?

The AI only prices items the complaint implies, using your labor and tax rates. Treat it as a fast starting point — final pricing depends on inspection, exactly like a quote written on paper.

How do I get it to the customer?

Download a branded PDF, copy a ready-to-text message, or create a share link they can open on their phone without logging in.
